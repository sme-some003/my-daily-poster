Privacy Policy - my_daily_poster

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Data we access

When the account owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives through TikTok's official API:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar)
  • Access and refresh tokens that allow the app to publish videos on the owner's behalf

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's own scheduled videos to their own TikTok account. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored securely on the owner's own device and are never shared, sold or transferred to third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the app is in use. Access can be revoked at any time via TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions), after which stored tokens are deleted.
